Money transfers let you move funds between accounts, send money to friends or family, and make payments quickly. In the U.S., there are several options for both domestic and international transfers, each with different costs and speeds.

## Domestic Transfers
- **Bank-to-Bank (ACH Transfers)**  
  - Free or low-cost, usually takes 1–3 business days.  
  - Common for moving money between your own accounts at different banks.  
- **Wire Transfers**  
  - Near-instant, but usually cost $15–$30 per transfer.  
  - Often used for large purchases (e.g., buying a car).  

---

## Peer-to-Peer Payment Apps
- **Zelle** – built into many bank apps, fast, usually free.  
- **Venmo** – popular for splitting bills, sending rent, or casual payments.  
- **Cash App** – simple for personal transfers; also offers a debit card.  
- **PayPal** – widely accepted, especially for online purchases.  

*Tip: Use apps carefully—transfers are often instant and can’t be reversed if you send to the wrong person.*  

---

## International Transfers
- **Banks**  
  - Offer international wire transfers.  
  - Reliable but usually expensive (high fees and poor exchange rates).  
- **Money Transfer Services**  
  - Examples: Western Union, MoneyGram, Wise (formerly TransferWise), Remitly.  
  - Often cheaper and faster than banks.  
  - Wise and similar services use mid-market exchange rates, making them more cost-effective.  
- **PayPal/Xoom**  
  - Convenient if you already use PayPal.  
  - Fees vary, especially with currency conversions.  

---

## Comparing Costs & Speed
- **Cheapest:** ACH transfers (domestic) and Wise (international).  
- **Fastest:** Wires (domestic or international) but with higher fees.  
- **Most convenient:** Peer-to-peer apps for small domestic payments.  

Always check:  
- Transfer fees.  
- Exchange rate markup (for international).  
- Delivery time (instant, same day, or multiple days).  

---

## Quick Tips
- For recurring payments (like rent), check if your landlord accepts **Zelle** or **ACH** instead of checks.  
- Avoid using credit cards for transfers—fees are high and treated as cash advances.  
- Compare services before sending money abroad—fees and rates vary widely.  
- Keep receipts or confirmations, especially for large or international transfers.
