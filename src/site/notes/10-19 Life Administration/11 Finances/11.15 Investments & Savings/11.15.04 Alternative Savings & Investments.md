## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- Bank accounts that lock in your money for a fixed term (e.g., 6 months, 1 year).  
- Offer higher interest rates than savings accounts.  
- Penalty fees apply if you withdraw before the maturity date.  
- Best for money you **know you won’t need soon**.  

---

## Money Market Accounts
- A blend between savings and checking accounts.  
- Typically require a higher balance.  
- Pay higher interest rates than standard savings.  
- May allow limited debit card use or check-writing.  

---

## Real Estate Basics
- Buying property as an investment can build long-term wealth.  
- Options include:  
  - Owning a rental property.  
  - Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) for easier access.  
- Downsides: high upfront cost, ongoing maintenance, market fluctuations.  

---

## High-Risk Investments
- Includes things like **cryptocurrency, day trading, penny stocks, and speculative assets**.  
- Potential for high returns, but also **high risk of loss**.  
- Not recommended until you have a strong financial foundation (emergency fund, retirement savings).  

---

## Quick Tips
- Use CDs or money markets for money you want safe but still growing.  
- Be cautious with real estate—consider your time, finances, and local market.  
- Treat high-risk investments as “extra,” not a replacement for core savings.
