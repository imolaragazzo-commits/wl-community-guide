## Common Types of Bills
- **Housing**: rent or mortgage.  
- **Utilities**: electricity, gas, water, trash, internet.  
- **Insurance**: health, auto, renters.  
- **Loans & Credit Cards**: minimum payments required monthly.  
- **Subscriptions**: streaming services, gyms, memberships.  

---

## Ways to Pay
- **Online Banking**: set up bill pay through your bank.  
- **Direct Website Payment**: pay directly on the company’s site.  
- **Checks**: less common now but sometimes still required.  
- **Cash/Money Orders**: used for certain landlords or government fees.  

---

## Autopay Basics
- Autopay lets a company automatically withdraw your payment on the due date.  
- Benefits:  
  - Avoids late fees.  
  - Reduces stress of remembering due dates.  
- Risks:  
  - Overdraft fees if your account doesn’t have enough funds.  
  - Harder to spot errors if you don’t check statements.  

---

## Staying Organized
- Keep a **list of due dates** in a calendar or app.  
- Set **reminders** a few days before each bill is due.  
- Review autopay charges monthly to catch mistakes.  
- Track subscriptions—you may be paying for unused services.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Autopay essentials (rent, utilities, insurance), but review optional subscriptions.  
- Align due dates with payday if possible.  
- If you’re new to autopay, start with one or two bills to build trust.
