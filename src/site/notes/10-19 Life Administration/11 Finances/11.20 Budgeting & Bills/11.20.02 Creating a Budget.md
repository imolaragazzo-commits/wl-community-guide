A budget is a plan for how you’ll spend and save your money. It helps you cover essentials, reduce debt, and set aside funds for future goals.

## Why Budgeting Matters
- Keeps spending aligned with income.  
- Prevents overspending on wants before needs are covered.  
- Helps build savings and reduce financial stress.  
- Provides a roadmap for reaching long-term goals.  

---

## Popular Budgeting Methods
- **50/30/20 Rule**:  
  - 50% Needs (housing, food, transportation).  
  - 30% Wants (entertainment, travel, extras).  
  - 20% Savings & Debt Repayment.  

- **Zero-Based Budget**:  
  - Every dollar is assigned a “job” (spending, saving, investing).  
  - Forces intentional choices.  

- **Envelope System**:  
  - Cash divided into envelopes for each category (groceries, gas, etc.).  
  - When the envelope is empty, you stop spending in that category.  

---

## Budgeting Tools
- Apps like **Mint**, **YNAB (You Need a Budget)**, and **EveryDollar** make tracking easy.  
- Spreadsheets are free and customizable.  
- Some banks offer built-in expense tracking and alerts.  

---

## Sticking to Your Budget
- Track spending regularly (weekly check-ins work best).  
- Adjust categories if you consistently overspend or underspend.  
- Automate savings so it happens before you can spend the money.  
- Review and update your budget monthly.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Start simple—don’t overwhelm yourself with too many categories.  
- Focus on **needs first, wants second**.  
- Be flexible; budgets evolve as your life changes.
