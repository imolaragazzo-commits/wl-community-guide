Final exams at Purdue take place during the week immediately following the end of classes. The schedule is centrally managed by the Registrar and depends on your class meeting times.

## When Finals Are Held
- Finals week is a **Monday–Saturday block** after the last week of classes.  
- Exams are typically **2 hours** long.  
- Evening exams may be scheduled between **7:00–9:00 PM**.  

---

## How the Schedule Is Determined
- Exam times are based on your **class’s regular meeting time**, not chosen by the instructor.  
- The Registrar publishes a matrix each semester assigning exam slots.  
- Some courses (especially large lectures) may schedule **common exams** at fixed times.  

[Final Exam Schedules – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/final-exams/)  

---

## Finding Your Final Exam Time
- Check the official schedule matrix each semester.  
- Your professors will confirm final exam details in class and on Brightspace.  
- Verify early to avoid travel conflicts—don’t book flights before exams are done.  

---

## Conflicts & Overloads
- A conflict occurs if you have **two exams at the same time**.  
- An overload occurs if you have **three or more exams in one day**.  
- Purdue allows you to request a reschedule through your instructor/department if either situation applies.  

[Final Exam Policies – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/final-exams/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Don’t assume finals are at your normal class time—**check the matrix**.  
- Book travel **after** your last exam; students have missed finals by leaving early.  
- Finals can be spread across six days—plan study time accordingly.  
- Large lecture finals may take place in big auditoriums, not your usual classroom.
