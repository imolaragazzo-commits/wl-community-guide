Purdue has firm deadlines each semester for adding, dropping, or withdrawing from courses. Knowing these dates ensures you avoid penalties and keep your academic record in good standing.

## Early Semester Deadlines
- **Add/Drop without a grade**: Typically the **first week** of classes.  
- **Registration changes** during this time do not appear on your transcript.  
- Tuition adjustments may still apply for dropped courses.  

---

## Mid-Semester Deadlines
- **Last day to withdraw with a “W”**: Around the **end of week 9** in a 16-week semester.  
- Dropping after this date is generally not allowed unless for extenuating circumstances.  
- Some programs or scholarships require full-time enrollment—check before withdrawing.  

---

## Late Semester Deadlines
- **Last day of classes**: Final cutoff for most academic changes.  
- **Graduation application deadlines** are also tied to late semester dates.  
- Dropping all classes at this stage counts as a **withdrawal from the university**, not just a course drop.  

---

## Where to Find Official Dates
The official list of deadlines is maintained by the Registrar:  
- [Registration Calendars – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registrationCalendars/)  

This page includes add/drop deadlines, withdrawal dates, and refund schedules for each semester.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Put key deadlines in your calendar at the start of every semester.  
- Dropping a class may affect **financial aid, scholarships, or visa status**.  
- Check with your advisor before making changes—sometimes alternatives (like pass/no pass) exist.
