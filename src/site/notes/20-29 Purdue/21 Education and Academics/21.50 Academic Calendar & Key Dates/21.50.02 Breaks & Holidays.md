## Fall Semester Breaks
- **Labor Day** – University closed (early September).  
- **Fall Break** – Usually two days in October.  
- **Thanksgiving Break** – Full week in late November.  

---

## Spring Semester Breaks
- **Martin Luther King Jr. Day** – University closed (mid-January).  
- **Spring Break** – One full week in March.  

---

## University Holidays
In addition to semester breaks, Purdue observes standard U.S. holidays when the university is closed:  
- **New Year’s Day**  
- **Memorial Day** (late May)  
- **Juneteenth** (June 19)  
- **Independence Day** (July 4)  
- **Labor Day** (September)  
- **Thanksgiving & Day After** (November)  
- **Christmas Day** (December 25)  

Official list here: [Purdue Academic Calendar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/calendars/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Breaks are a good time to travel home, but be careful with booking flights around **final exam weeks**.  
- Campus dining courts and buses may run on **reduced schedules** during breaks—plan ahead.  
- International students: some breaks are short; factor travel time and costs before planning trips.
