Purdue runs on a semester system with **Fall, Spring, and Summer** terms. Each has fixed start and end dates published well in advance. Knowing these dates helps with planning travel, internships, and academic workload.

## Fall Semester
- Begins in **late August**.  
- Runs for **16 weeks**, ending in mid-December.  
- Includes a one-week **Thanksgiving Break**.  
- Final exams occur the week after classes end.  

---

## Spring Semester
- Begins in **early January**.  
- Runs for **16 weeks**, ending in early May.  
- Includes a one-week **Spring Break** in March.  
- Final exams occur the week after classes end.  

---

## Summer Term
- Divided into multiple modules:  
  - **Maymester** (4 weeks, intensive).  
  - **Summer Session I & II** (8 weeks each).  
  - **Full Summer Session** (12 weeks).  
- Great for catching up or accelerating your degree progress.  

---

## Where to Find Official Dates
Purdue publishes the academic calendar several years in advance:  
- [Purdue Academic Calendar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/calendars/)  

This site includes semester start/end dates, holidays, and final exam schedules.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Always check the **official academic calendar** before booking travel—exams often end later than expected.  
- Summer classes are compressed; expect faster pacing.  
- Faculty may adjust within the semester dates, but start and end dates are fixed.
