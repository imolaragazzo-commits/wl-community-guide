## When Commencement Happens
- Purdue holds commencement **three times per year**:  
  - **Spring** (May)  
  - **Summer** (August)  
  - **Winter** (December)  
- Each ceremony is tied to the academic calendar and takes place after finals week.  

---

## Spring, Summer & Winter Ceremonies
- **Spring**: Largest ceremony, divided into multiple sessions by college.  
- **Summer**: Smaller ceremony, typically one session for all colleges.  
- **Winter**: Mid-size ceremony, divided by college if needed.  

---

## Finding Your Ceremony Date
- Specific dates and times are posted each semester on the Commencement site.  
- Students register for commencement in advance to confirm participation.  
- Families should plan travel only after confirming official dates.  

[Commencement – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/commencement/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Sign up early—registration deadlines for commencement come **weeks before finals**.  
- Double-check your assigned ceremony session; it depends on your **college/major**.  
- If you’re not attending, you can still receive your diploma by mail.  
- Hotels in West Lafayette fill quickly around commencement—reserve well in advance.
