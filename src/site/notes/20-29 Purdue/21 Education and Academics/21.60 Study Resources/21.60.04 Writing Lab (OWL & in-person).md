## About the Writing Lab
- Located in **Heavilon Hall, Room 226**.  
- Open to all Purdue students, faculty, and staff.  
- Services are **free** and included in tuition.  
- Offers both general writing support and specialized help for courses or career prep.  

[Writing Lab – Purdue](https://cla.purdue.edu/academic/english/writing-lab/)  

---

## In-Person Services
- One-on-one tutoring sessions with trained writing consultants.  
- Bring drafts, outlines, or even just ideas.  
- Appointments can cover essays, lab reports, research papers, resumes, or grad school applications.  
- Book through **BoilerConnect**.  

[BoilerConnect](https://www.purdue.edu/boilerconnect/)  

---

## Online Writing Lab (OWL)
- One of the most widely used writing resources in the world.  
- Offers free guides on:  
  - MLA, APA, and Chicago citation styles  
  - Grammar and mechanics  
  - Professional writing (cover letters, resumes)  
  - Research strategies  
- Available anytime, no login required.  

[Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Workshops & Specialized Help
- Group workshops on writing basics, research skills, and avoiding plagiarism.  
- Discipline-specific writing support (e.g., engineering reports, business communication).  
- ESL (English as a Second Language) help for non-native English speakers.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Don’t wait until the night before—it’s better to visit the Writing Lab during the drafting stage.  
- Bring assignment prompts with you so consultants can align feedback with instructor expectations.  
- Use the OWL for quick citation questions—it’s faster than flipping through a manual.
