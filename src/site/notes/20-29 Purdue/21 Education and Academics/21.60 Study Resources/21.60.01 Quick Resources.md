## Tutoring & Writing Help
- **Academic Success Center (ASC)**: free tutoring, supplemental instruction, and workshops.  
- **Writing Lab (OWL & in-person)**: help with essays, resumes, and research papers.  
[Academic Success Center](https://www.purdue.edu/asc/)  
[Writing Lab (OWL)](https://owl.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Community & External Resources
These unofficial tools are built by students or the community. They can be extremely useful, but always double-check with official resources for accuracy.  

- **BoilerGrades** – crowd-sourced grade distributions to compare classes.  
[BoilerGrades](https://www.boilergrades.com/)  

- **BoilerSchedule** – alternative scheduling interface to build mock timetables.  
[BoilerSchedule](https://boilerschedule.com/)  

- **BoilerClasses** – searchable course catalog with filters and reviews.  
[BoilerClasses](https://boilerclasses.com/)  

- **BoilerExams** – archive of past exams and study materials shared by students.  
[BoilerExams](https://www.boilerexams.com/)  

- **Chenflix** – collection of recorded lecture resources for certain Purdue courses.  
[Chenflix](https://sites.google.com/view/chenflix/home)  

- **RateMyProfessors** – reviews of instructors from Purdue and elsewhere (take with caution).  
[RateMyProfessors](https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/)  

- **r/Purdue subreddit** – student-run forum where course and professor discussions come up frequently.  
[r/Purdue on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Purdue/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Bookmark both official and community tools—you’ll likely use a mix.  
- Use past exam archives like **BoilerExams** to practice under real conditions.  
- Community tools are great for planning and study help, but confirm requirements with your advisor or syllabus.
