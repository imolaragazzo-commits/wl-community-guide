Office hours are one of the most underused academic resources at Purdue. They’re set times when professors and teaching assistants are available to answer questions, review material, and provide guidance outside of class.

## What Are Office Hours?
- Regularly scheduled times posted by instructors for student drop-ins.  
- Usually listed on the syllabus or announced in class.  
- Can be held in person, virtually, or both.  

---

## Why They Matter
- Direct access to your instructor or TA outside of lecture.  
- Chance to clarify difficult concepts before exams.  
- Builds relationships that may lead to mentorship, research opportunities, or recommendation letters.  
- Demonstrates initiative and engagement.  

---

## How to Prepare
- Review class notes and try the homework before going.  
- Write down specific questions or problems you’re stuck on.  
- Bring your textbook, assignment, or laptop for context.  

---

## What to Ask
- Clarification on lecture material or readings.  
- Help with homework problems (but not asking for full solutions).  
- Guidance on study strategies for upcoming exams.  
- Feedback on papers, lab reports, or projects.  
- Advice on careers, research, or graduate school.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Go early in the semester—not just before exams.  
- If the office hours are busy, ask short, focused questions.  
- Be respectful of other students’ time—don’t monopolize the session.  
- TAs often run office hours too; they can be just as helpful as professors.
