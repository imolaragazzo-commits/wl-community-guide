## Academic Success Center Overview
- Central hub for academic support at Purdue.  
- Provides tutoring, study groups, SI sessions, and skill-building workshops.  
- Located in **Wilmeth Active Learning Center (WALC)**.  

[Academic Success Center – Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/asc/)  

---

## Supplemental Instruction (SI)
- Peer-led study sessions for historically challenging courses (e.g., Chemistry, Biology, Math).  
- Sessions focus on problem-solving and collaborative learning.  
- Attendance is optional but highly recommended for first-year STEM courses.  
- Led by trained upperclassmen who previously excelled in the course.  

[Supplemental Instruction – ASC](https://www.purdue.edu/asc/programs/si.html)  

---

## Tutoring Services
- Free, drop-in tutoring for a variety of subjects (STEM-heavy).  
- Tutors are trained undergraduates or graduate students.  
- Available in group settings—great for working through homework or exam prep.  
- Some departments (like Computer Science and Chemistry) also run their own tutoring centers.  

[Free Tutoring – ASC](https://www.purdue.edu/asc/programs/dropintutoring.html)  

---

## Workshops & Academic Coaching
- ASC offers workshops on:  
  - Time management  
  - Note-taking strategies  
  - Test prep and stress management  
- One-on-one **academic coaching** is also available by appointment for personalized support.  

[Workshops & Coaching – ASC](https://www.purdue.edu/asc/programs/workshops.html)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Check the SI schedule early—sessions often conflict with labs or recitations.  
- Go to SI or tutoring **before** you fall behind; it’s easier to keep up than to catch up.  
- Bring specific questions to tutoring; you’ll get more out of it than starting cold.
