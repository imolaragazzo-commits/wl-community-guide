## Major Libraries on Campus
- **Hicks Undergraduate Library (HIKS)** – 24-hour access during exam weeks, group study rooms, very central.  
- **Humanities, Social Science & Education Library (HSSE)** – historic atmosphere, located in Stewart Center.  
- **Wilmeth Active Learning Center (WALC)** – largest, modern space with classrooms by day and open study space at night.  

[University Libraries – Purdue](https://www.lib.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Specialized Libraries
- **Aviation Technology Library** – for aviation and aerospace resources.  
- **Math Sciences Library** – math-focused texts and journals.  
- **Life Sciences Library** – housed in Lilly Hall, supports biological sciences.  
- **Chemistry Library** – Chaney-Hale Hall of Science.  

[Subject Libraries – Purdue](https://www.lib.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Popular Study Spaces
Outside of the libraries, Purdue has several dedicated study-friendly spots:  
- **PMU (Purdue Memorial Union)** – central gathering place, study lounges, and food options.  
- **CoRec (France A. Córdova Recreational Center)** – some quiet lounges for casual studying.  
- **Residence Halls** – many have study lounges on each floor.  
- **Krannert/Rawls Hall Atrium** – great for group work with natural light.  

---

## Quiet vs. Collaborative Areas
- Libraries are usually divided into **quiet floors** (individual work) and **collaborative zones** (group study).  
- WALC and Hicks offer bookable **study rooms** for small groups.  
- Stewart Center and PMU have tucked-away corners that are often overlooked.  

---

## Quick Tips
- During finals, arrive early—WALC and Hicks fill up quickly.  
- Book group study rooms ahead of time online.  
- Rotate your study spots; a change of scenery helps with focus.  
- Noise-canceling headphones are your friend in open areas.
