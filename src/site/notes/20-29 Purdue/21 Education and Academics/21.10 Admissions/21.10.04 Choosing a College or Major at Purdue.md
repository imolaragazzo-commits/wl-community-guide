## Overview of Colleges & Schools
Purdue’s majors are organized into colleges/schools, such as:  
- College of Engineering  
- College of Science  
- College of Liberal Arts  
- Krannert School of Management  
- College of Health & Human Sciences  
- College of Agriculture  
- College of Education  
- College of Pharmacy  
- College of Veterinary Medicine  
- Polytechnic Institute  

Explore the full list here: [Purdue Majors & Minors](https://www.purdue.edu/majors/)  

---

## Selecting a Major on the Application
- You’ll list a **first-choice** and **second-choice** major.  
- Your application is reviewed **by college/major**, not just by Purdue as a whole.  
- A well-chosen second-choice major can improve your chances of admission if your first-choice fills quickly.  

[How to Apply – Purdue Admissions](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)

## Competitive & Restricted Majors
Some majors are **capacity-limited** and much more competitive:  
- Engineering (First-Year Engineering program)  
- Nursing  
- Computer Science  
- Professional Flight  

Applying by the **priority deadline (Nov 1)** is critical for these majors. Late applications are rarely considered.  

[Freshman Admissions Criteria – Purdue Admissions](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/freshman/)

## Changing Your Major Later (CODO)
If you start in one major and later want to move to another, you’ll go through Purdue’s **CODO process** (Change of Degree Objective).  
- Each program has specific GPA, course, and credit requirements.  
- Admission is not guaranteed—competitive majors may rarely take CODO students.  
- Meet with your academic advisor early to plan ahead.  

[CODO Requirements – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/students/ChangeCurriculum.html)  

---

## Quick Tips
- If you’re interested in Engineering, everyone starts in **First-Year Engineering** before declaring a specific discipline.  
- Consider choosing a less selective **second-choice major** in the same field to keep doors open.  
- Read the **CODO requirements** for your dream major before you enroll—it helps avoid surprises later.
