## First-Time College Students (Domestic Freshmen)
- Apply via the **Common App** for the West Lafayette or Indianapolis campuses. Purdue offers over 200 majors. [Purdue Admissions – Apply](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)  
- A complete application often includes: the application itself (with essay and Purdue-specific questions), self-reported high school grades, ACT or SAT scores (optional but recommended), and the application fee (or waiver). [Purdue Admissions – Guide](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/guide/)  

---

## International Students
- Most international students also apply through the **Common App**, but must additionally submit proof of English proficiency, certified translated transcripts, and financial documentation for visa purposes. [Purdue Admissions – Guide](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/guide/)  

---

## Transfer Students
- Students who have completed **12+ college-level credits post-high school** may apply as transfers using Purdue’s portal. [Purdue Admissions – Apply](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)  
- Before applying, check the **Closed Programs** list to see if your intended major is currently unavailable. [Purdue Admissions – Transfer](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/transfer/)  

---

## Re-entry & Other Applicants
- **Re-entry** applies to students who previously attended Purdue and now wish to return. This follows a separate process, not the Common App. [Purdue Admissions – Apply](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)  
- **Non-degree seeking** applicants (e.g., guests, dual-credit, lifelong learners) follow a different application path. [Purdue Admissions – Apply](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)  
- **Military-affiliated** or special population applicants may find tailored information under Purdue’s admissions “Special Populations” section. [Purdue Admissions – Apply](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/apply/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Double-check your application path early—forms, documents, and deadlines vary by path.  
- International applicants should account for additional visa processing time after admission.  
- **Transfer applicants**: consult the Closed Programs list—many competitive majors don’t accept transfers every term; this can save effort.
