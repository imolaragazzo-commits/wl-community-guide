## First-Year Applicants
- **Application opens**: August 1 (Common App).  
- **Priority deadline**: November 1  
  - Best chance for admission to competitive majors (Engineering, Nursing, Computer Science).  
  - Required to be considered for merit scholarships and the Honors College.  
- **Regular deadline**: January 15  
- **FAFSA priority filing deadline**: April 15 (Indiana residents).  

[See Purdue Admissions – Deadlines](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/freshman/deadlines.php)  

---

## Transfer Applicants
- Applications are accepted for fall, spring, and summer terms.  
- Each term has a set **priority deadline**—typically:  
  - Fall: March 1  
  - Spring: November 1  
  - Summer: May 1  
- Some majors may close earlier due to capacity. Always check the **Closed Programs** list before applying.  

[Purdue Admissions – Transfer Deadlines](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/transfer/)  

---

## International Applicants
- International deadlines align with first-year and transfer timelines.  
- Allow extra time for:  
  - **English proficiency tests** (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo).  
  - **Visa paperwork** once admitted.  
- Strongly recommended: submit by the **priority deadline** to ensure enough time for immigration processes.  

[Purdue Admissions – International Applicants](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/international/)  

---

## Decision Release Dates
- **Early Action decisions**: mid-January (for applications submitted by November 1).  
- **Regular Decision notifications**: March.  
- Transfer timelines vary; most decisions are released on a rolling basis.  

[Purdue Admissions – Freshman Decisions](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/freshman/deadlines.php)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Apply by **November 1** if you want maximum options: scholarships, Honors College, and competitive majors.  
- Double-check deadlines for your **specific major**—some fill up early and close before general deadlines.  
- Transfer students: apply well ahead of the deadline; space can run out.
