## First-Year Applicants
- **Application** via the Common App with Purdue-specific questions.  
- **Application essay** (Common App personal statement).  
- **High school transcript** (self-reported, then official copy if admitted).  
- **Standardized test scores** (ACT or SAT; test optional for many applicants, but can strengthen certain applications).  
- **Application fee** or fee waiver.  

[Purdue Admissions – Freshman Application Requirements](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/freshman/)  

---

## International Applicants
In addition to freshman requirements:  
- **Proof of English proficiency** (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo English Test).  
- **Official academic records** with certified English translations if originals are not in English.  
- **Financial documentation** to demonstrate ability to cover costs for visa eligibility.  

[Purdue Admissions – International Students](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/international/)  

---

## Transfer Applicants
- **Purdue transfer application** (via Purdue’s portal, not Common App).  
- **Official transcripts** from all colleges/universities attended.  
- **High school transcript** (sometimes required, especially if fewer than 24 college credits completed).  
- **Major-specific requirements** may include higher GPA thresholds or prerequisite courses.  

[Purdue Admissions – Transfer Students](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/transfer/)  

---

## Optional & Recommended Items
- **Letters of recommendation** – not required, but can add context (especially for scholarships or special programs).  
- **Resume or activity list** – gives detail on leadership, service, and extracurriculars.  
- **Additional essays** – required for the Honors College and some scholarship programs.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Send official transcripts directly from your school or testing service. Self-uploaded copies aren’t accepted for final admission.  
- Test optional means you can decide whether to include ACT/SAT scores, but Purdue will consider them if submitted.  
- International students: check that your **name matches exactly** across passport, transcripts, and application.
