Participation in commencement requires a separate application process in addition to applying for graduation. Students also need to order official regalia (cap, gown, and hood if applicable) to take part in the ceremony.

## Commencement Registration
- After applying for graduation, you must separately **register for commencement**.  
- Registration is done in [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/) under the **Destination: Graduation** tab.  
- You’ll confirm:  
  - Whether you plan to attend in person or not.  
  - How you want your name announced and printed in the program.  
  - Guest ticket requests.  

[Commencement Registration – Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/commencement/)  

---

## Ordering Regalia
- Regalia (cap, gown, tassel, and hood for advanced degrees) is required to participate.  
- Order through Purdue’s approved vendor, **Herff Jones**.  
- Regalia can be shipped to you or picked up on campus.  

[Order Regalia – Purdue Commencement](https://www.purdue.edu/commencement/regalia/)  

---

## Costs & Deadlines
- Regalia costs vary by degree level (undergraduate, master’s, doctoral).  
- Prices typically range from **$60–$120**.  
- Deadlines for ordering are strict—usually about **6–8 weeks before the ceremony**.  
- Late rentals may have limited availability or extra fees.  

---

## Diplomas & Ceremony Attendance
- Diplomas are mailed to your address after final grades and degree certification.  
- Attendance at commencement is optional—you’ll still earn your degree if you don’t attend.  
- If you can’t attend, you may still order regalia for photos or celebrations.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Register for commencement as soon as the portal opens—guest ticket numbers are limited.  
- Double-check your **diploma mailing address** in myPurdue.  
- Keep regalia in good shape—you’ll likely want it for photos with family and friends.
