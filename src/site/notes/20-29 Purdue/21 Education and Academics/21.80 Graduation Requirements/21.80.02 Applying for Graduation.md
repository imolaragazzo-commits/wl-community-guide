Graduating from Purdue isn’t automatic—you must apply for graduation through the Registrar. The process ensures your degree audit is completed and that you’re included in commencement if you choose to participate.

## When to Apply
- Applications open **months before graduation**.  
- Deadlines are posted on the Registrar’s site and vary by semester.  
- Apply during the semester **before you plan to graduate** to avoid delays.  

[Graduation Deadlines – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/graduation/deadlines.html)  

---

## How to Apply
1. Log into [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/).  
2. Navigate to the **Academic tab → Destination: Graduation**.  
3. Complete the **Graduation Application** form.  
4. Verify your **diploma name and mailing address**.  
5. Submit by the posted deadline.  

[Apply to Graduate – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/graduation/applying.html)  

---

## Degree Audit & Approval
- Once you apply, your college conducts a **senior check** using MyPurduePlan/DegreeWorks.  
- Any missing requirements must be completed before final approval.  
- If issues arise, your advisor will notify you.  

---

## Commencement Participation
- Applying for graduation is separate from signing up for **commencement ceremonies**.  
- You’ll need to register for commencement later if you plan to attend.  
- If you’re not attending, your diploma will be mailed after degree conferral.  

[Commencement Information – Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/commencement/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Apply even if you’re unsure you’ll graduate on time—you can withdraw later if needed.  
- Double-check your **diploma name**; it must match legal or preferred name guidelines.  
- Late applications may delay your diploma or exclude you from commencement.
