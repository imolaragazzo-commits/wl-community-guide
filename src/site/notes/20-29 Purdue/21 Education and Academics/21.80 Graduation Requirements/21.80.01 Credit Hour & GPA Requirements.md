## Credit Hour Requirements
- Most bachelor’s degrees require **120–130 credit hours**.  
- Certain professional programs (Pharmacy, Engineering, Veterinary Medicine) may require more.  
- Credit hours include major courses, electives, and core curriculum requirements.  

[Degree Requirements – Purdue Catalog](https://catalog.purdue.edu/)  

---

## GPA Requirements
- A **minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0** is required to graduate.  
- Some programs set higher minimum GPAs (e.g., Nursing, Education, Engineering).  
- Honors designations require higher GPAs:  
  - **Cum Laude**: 3.30–3.59  
  - **Magna Cum Laude**: 3.60–3.79  
  - **Summa Cum Laude**: 3.80–4.00  

---

## Residency Requirement
- At least **32 credit hours** must be completed at Purdue (on the West Lafayette campus or approved Purdue system program).  
- This ensures a significant portion of your degree is earned directly from Purdue faculty.  

---

## Program-Specific Standards
- Colleges and departments may impose additional requirements:  
  - Certain GPAs in major-specific courses.  
  - Completion of “gateway” or capstone courses.  
  - Successful completion of senior design, thesis, or practicum.  
- Always check your **MyPurduePlan/DegreeWorks** to confirm progress.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Don’t assume electives will “just count”—verify they meet credit hour needs.  
- Keep an eye on **credit-heavy classes** (5+ credits); they can heavily impact GPA.  
- Work with your advisor on a **senior check** to avoid last-minute surprises.
