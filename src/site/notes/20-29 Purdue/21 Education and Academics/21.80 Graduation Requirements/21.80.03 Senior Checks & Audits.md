Before graduating, every Purdue student must complete a **senior check** (sometimes called a degree audit). This ensures all credit hours, GPA requirements, and program-specific courses are on track before your final semester.

## What is a Senior Check?
- A review of your **MyPurduePlan/DegreeWorks** to verify graduation eligibility.  
- Conducted by your **academic advisor** or college’s student services office.  
- Identifies missing requirements early so you can adjust your final schedule.  

---

## When It Happens
- Typically during the **semester before you graduate**.  
- Some colleges require a check when you reach **90+ credit hours**.  
- The senior check often ties into your **graduation application**.  

---

## What Advisors Look For
- Total credit hours (usually 120–130 minimum).  
- Completion of all **core curriculum** and **major requirements**.  
- GPA thresholds for graduation and any honors.  
- Transfer credits and substitutions properly recorded.  
- In-progress courses needed to finish on time.  

---

## How to Prepare
- Run your own **DegreeWorks audit** before meeting with your advisor.  
- Bring questions about electives, minors, or certificates.  
- Confirm any transfer or study abroad credits have been posted.  
- If issues appear, work with your advisor immediately to adjust.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Don’t assume everything is logged correctly—clerical errors happen.  
- Ask about how dropping or failing a class will affect your graduation status.  
- Use the senior check as a chance to discuss **career or grad school timelines**.
