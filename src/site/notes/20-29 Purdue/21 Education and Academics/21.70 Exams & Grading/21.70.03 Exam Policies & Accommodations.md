## General Exam Policies
- Exams must be held at the **time and place announced** by the instructor.  
- Instructors are required to give **at least one week’s notice** before in-class exams.  
- Academic integrity applies: using unauthorized aids, collaborating without permission, or cheating may result in disciplinary action.  

[Academic Regulations – Purdue Catalog](https://catalog.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Final Exam Policies
- Final exams are scheduled by the **Registrar**, not the instructor.  
- The official **Final Exam Schedule Matrix** assigns times based on class meeting patterns.  
- Students with **three or more finals in one day** may request rescheduling.  

[Final Exam Schedules – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/final-exams/)  

---

## Make-Up Exams
- Allowed for excused absences (illness, family emergency, university-sponsored event).  
- Students should notify instructors **as soon as possible** and may need documentation.  
- Instructors may set the make-up exam format (written, oral, or online).  

---

## Accommodations & DRC
- Students with disabilities can arrange exam accommodations through the **Disability Resource Center (DRC)**.  
- Common accommodations include:  
  - Extended time  
  - Reduced-distraction testing environment  
  - Assistive technology  
- Students must:  
  1. Register with the DRC.  
  2. Share their **accommodation letters** with instructors.  
  3. Coordinate exam logistics in advance.  

[Disability Resource Center – Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/drc/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Always check the **syllabus** for each course’s exam policies.  
- Confirm final exam times early—don’t book travel until after exams.  
- If you need accommodations, reach out to the DRC **at least a few weeks before exams**.
