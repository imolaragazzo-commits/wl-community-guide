Grades at Purdue follow a standard **A–F scale** with corresponding grade points. Your GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated by weighting these grade points against the number of credit hours you’ve completed.

## Grading Scale
Purdue’s official undergraduate grading system:  

| Grade | Points per Credit Hour |
|-------|-------------------------|
| A     | 4.0 |
| A-    | 3.7 |
| B+    | 3.3 |
| B     | 3.0 |
| B-    | 2.7 |
| C+    | 2.3 |
| C     | 2.0 |
| C-    | 1.7 |
| D+    | 1.3 |
| D     | 1.0 |
| D-    | 0.7 |
| F     | 0.0 |  

Some courses (labs, seminars) may use **S/U (Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory)** grading.  

[Grading System – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/grades/)

## Calculating GPA
1. Multiply the grade points for each class by the number of credit hours.  
2. Add these totals together.  
3. Divide by the total number of credit hours attempted.  

**Example:**  
- ENGL 10600 (4 credits) = B (3.0 × 4 = 12.0)  
- MA 16100 (5 credits) = A (4.0 × 5 = 20.0)  
- BIOL 11000 (3 credits) = C+ (2.3 × 3 = 6.9)  

Total = 38.9 grade points ÷ 12 credits = **3.24 GPA**

## Repeated Courses
- If you retake a course, **both grades remain on your transcript**.  
- GPA uses the **most recent grade** for calculation.  
- Some programs still consider all attempts for prerequisite requirements.  

---

## Pass/No Pass Option
- Available for some elective courses outside your major requirements.  
- **P = Pass** (credit earned, no GPA impact).  
- **N = No Pass** (no credit, no GPA impact).  
- Not allowed for major/core courses.  
- Must be elected early in the semester—check Registrar deadlines.  

[Pass/No Pass Policy – Purdue Catalog](https://catalog.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- A “C” is generally the minimum for courses that are prerequisites to higher-level classes.  
- Watch how **credit-heavy courses** affect GPA—a bad grade in a 5-credit class hits harder.  
- Run GPA calculations in **MyPurduePlan/DegreeWorks** to see how future grades will impact your standing.
