Boiler Gold Rush (BGR) is Purdue’s official orientation week, held before the fall semester begins. It’s designed to help new students transition to college life, build community, and get comfortable navigating campus. **BGRi** is a special track for international students, starting a few days earlier.

## What is Boiler Gold Rush?
- A week of activities, tours, and events to introduce students to Purdue.  
- You’ll be placed in a **small team** led by student **Team Leaders**.  
- Events include campus tours, resource fairs, guest speakers, and social events.  
- Helps you meet people before classes begin and learn Purdue traditions.  

[Boiler Gold Rush – Purdue Orientation Programs](https://www.purdue.edu/orientation/bgr/)  

---

## BGRi for International Students
- Starts **a few days earlier** than regular BGR.  
- Includes sessions on cultural adjustment, immigration compliance, banking, and other life-in-the-US essentials.  
- Gives international students time to settle in before the larger orientation begins.  

[Boiler Gold Rush International (BGRi)](https://www.purdue.edu/orientation/bgri/)  

---

## Schedule & Activities
Typical BGR activities include:  
- Residence hall move-in and welcome events.  
- “Meet Purdue” sessions with academic advisors, resource staff, and student organizations.  
- Campus tours to learn where classes, libraries, and dining halls are.  
- Evening events like social mixers, concerts, and movie nights.  
- A group trip to **Ross-Ade Stadium** for the traditional kickoff.  

Schedules are posted each summer: [Purdue Orientation – Schedule](https://www.purdue.edu/orientation/bgr/schedule.php)  

---

## Registration & Costs
- **Registration is required** to attend BGR/BGRi.  
- Fee (varies each year; typically a few hundred dollars) covers materials, events, and staffing.  
- Financial assistance is available for students with demonstrated need.  

[Register for BGR](https://www.purdue.edu/orientation/bgr/registration.php)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Sign up early—spots are limited, and housing/move-in times are tied to BGR registration.  
- Pack for both indoor sessions and lots of walking outdoors.  
- Don’t skip the evening events—they’re where a lot of friendships start.  
- International students: use BGRi to get your practical setup (banking, phone, SSN info) out of the way.
