## Credit Hour & GPA Requirements
- Most bachelor’s degrees require **120–130 credit hours**.  
- Students must maintain a **2.0 cumulative GPA** to stay in good standing.  
- Certain majors (e.g., Engineering, Nursing, Pharmacy) may require higher GPAs for continuation.  
- At least **32 credit hours** must be completed at Purdue (residency requirement).  

[Purdue Academic Regulations](https://catalog.purdue.edu/content.php?catoid=16&navoid=20157)

## Core Curriculum
All Purdue undergraduates complete the **Core Curriculum**, which includes:  
- Written Communication  
- Oral Communication  
- Quantitative Reasoning  
- Science  
- Humanities  
- Social Sciences  
- Information Literacy  
- Science, Technology, & Society  
- Embedded outcomes (integrated into major coursework).  

[Core Curriculum – Purdue Undergraduate Education](https://www.purdue.edu/provost/students/s-initiatives/coreCurriculum/)

## Using MyPurduePlan
- Found in the **myPurdue portal**.  
- Tracks courses completed, in progress, and still required.  
- Color-coded blocks show if requirements are met, in progress, or missing.  
- Allows you to run **“What-If” audits** to see how credits would apply if you changed majors.  

[MyPurdue – Student Portal](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Using DegreeWorks
- Integrated into **MyPurduePlan**.  
- Provides an interactive audit of your academic progress.  
- Lets you plan out future semesters with “Planner” functionality.  
- Advisors often review your DegreeWorks audit with you during appointments.  

[DegreeWorks Overview – Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/students/degreeworks.html)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Check your **MyPurduePlan** every semester to make sure you’re on track.  
- Run a **“What-If” audit** before committing to a new major or minor.  
- Don’t assume electives will “just count”—make sure they slot into your degree plan.
