Here is a quick list of references
# Education
- [Boiler Classes](https://boilerclasses.com/): BoilerClasses is a faster and sleeker course catalogue for classes at Purdue University. It gives options for students to search by name, course code, professors, and more, all while providing a more intuitive interface than Purdue's official course catalogue.
- [Boiler Exams](https://www.boilerexams.com/): References to help study for exams for many classes.
- [ChenFlix](https://chenflix.vercel.app/): ChenFlix is a tool to help students study all forms of mathematics at Purdue! Created by Professor Chen, Purdue's assistant math department head, his site contains multiple semesters worth of lectures and notes dating as far back as 2016, all for free.
- [Boiler Grades](https://www.boilergrades.com/): Boiler Grades is an open-source site that uses publicly available data from Purdue to help students identify the average GPA correlated with professors and courses at Purdue.
