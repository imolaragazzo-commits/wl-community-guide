Purdue uses a **time ticket system** to stagger when students can register for classes. Your assigned window is based on credit hours earned and class standing, which helps spread out demand on the registration system.

## What is a Time Ticket?
- A **time ticket** is the specific date and time when you can begin registering for classes.  
- Registration windows are spread over several days to avoid everyone registering at once.  
- You cannot add courses in myPurdue until your time ticket opens.  

---

## How Time Tickets Are Assigned
- Determined by **total credit hours earned**, including transfer and AP/IB credits.  
- Seniors register first, followed by juniors, sophomores, and then freshmen.  
- Within each class level, priority may be given based on special groups (e.g., Honors, athletes, ROTC).  

---

## Finding Your Time Ticket
- Log into [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/).  
- Go to the **Registration tab → Registration Status**.  
- Your time ticket will show the exact **date and time** your registration window opens.  
- Time tickets are usually posted a few weeks before registration begins.  

[Registration Status – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registeringCourses/)  

---

## Using Your Window Effectively
- Prepare multiple schedule options in **Schedule Builder** before your ticket opens.  
- Log in **right at your assigned time**—popular courses fill within minutes.  
- Keep CRNs ready in case Schedule Builder crashes or lags.  
- If a class is full, add yourself to the waitlist or choose a backup.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Double-check your **time zone**—tickets are posted in Eastern Time.  
- Plan ahead: advisor meetings and holds must be cleared before registration opens.  
- Treat it like buying concert tickets—be ready to click when the window opens.
