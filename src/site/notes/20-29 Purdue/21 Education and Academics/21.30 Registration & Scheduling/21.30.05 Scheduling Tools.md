## Schedule Builder
- Integrated into [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/).  
- Lets you search for classes, build multiple schedule options, and load directly into registration.  
- Color-coded calendar view makes it easy to avoid time conflicts.  
- Highly recommended to prepare before your time ticket opens.  

[Schedule Builder – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registeringCourses/)  

---

## Look Up Classes
- Found under the **Registration tab** in myPurdue.  
- Traditional course search tool:  
  - Filter by subject, CRN, instructor, meeting time, or course attributes.  
  - Shows seat availability and waitlist status.  
- Less visual than Schedule Builder, but useful for quick CRN lookups.  

---

## Unofficial Tools
- **RateMyProfessors** – student reviews of instructors (take with a grain of salt).  
- **Reddit (r/Purdue)** – course recommendations and sample schedules.  
- **Student-made spreadsheets/trackers** – often shared in group chats or Discords.  
- These can give extra perspective but aren’t official sources.  

---

## Tips for Smarter Scheduling
- Balance **hard courses** with lighter electives to avoid burnout.  
- Spread out labs, recitations, and lectures to give yourself breaks.  
- Avoid scheduling all classes on one day—can be exhausting.  
- Morning vs evening—know your energy peaks.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Always have **backup CRNs** in case your first-choice schedule fills.  
- Don’t forget to include study blocks, meals, and extracurriculars.  
- Use MyPurduePlan/DegreeWorks to double-check courses meet degree requirements.
