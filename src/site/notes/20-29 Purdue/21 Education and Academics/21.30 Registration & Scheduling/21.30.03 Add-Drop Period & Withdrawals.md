Purdue offers flexibility to adjust your schedule after registration. The **add/drop period** lets you swap courses at the start of the semester, while **withdrawal** options exist if you need to step back later. Knowing the deadlines is key—dates are firm.

## Add/Drop Period
- Runs during the **first week of each semester**.  
- You can add, swap, or drop courses without a grade or transcript record.  
- All changes are made through [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/).  

[Add/Drop Deadlines – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registrationCalendars/)  

---

## Late Add/Drop
- After the first week, you’ll need **instructor and advisor approval** to add or drop a class.  
- A **“W” grade** may appear on your transcript for dropped classes after the first week.  
- Fees may apply for late adjustments.  

---

## Course Withdrawal (Single Class)
- Dropping a course after the add/drop period is considered a withdrawal.  
- Key points:  
  - Results in a grade of **“W”** on your transcript.  
  - Does not affect GPA, but counts as attempted credits.  
  - Deadlines for withdrawing vary each semester.  
- Always confirm how it affects your **full-time status**, financial aid, and scholarships.  

[Withdrawal Information – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registrationCalendars/)  

---

## Semester Withdrawal (All Classes)
- Withdrawing from all classes is considered a **withdrawal from the university**.  
- Requires meeting with an advisor and filling out official forms.  
- Impacts housing, financial aid, and immigration status (for international students).  
- Re-entry to Purdue later requires a separate **readmission application**.  

[Withdrawal Procedures – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registrationCalendars/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Mark the **last day to drop without a W** on your calendar—it sneaks up fast.  
- Talk to your advisor before dropping; sometimes other solutions exist (tutoring, grading options).  
- International students: dropping below 12 credit hours can affect visa status.
