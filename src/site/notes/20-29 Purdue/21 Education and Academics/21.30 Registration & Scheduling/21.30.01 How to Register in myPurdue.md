Course registration at Purdue happens through the **myPurdue portal**. It’s where you’ll build your schedule, add or drop classes, and confirm enrollment each semester.

## Accessing myPurdue
- Go to [myPurdue](https://mypurdue.purdue.edu/) and log in with your Purdue career account credentials.  
- Registration periods are based on **time tickets** (your assigned window to register).  
- Before registration opens, you’ll want to:  
  - Meet with your **academic advisor**.  
  - Clear any **holds** on your account (financial, advising, immunization).  

---

## Using the Registration Tools
- Inside myPurdue, select the **Registration tab**.  
- Two main tools are available:  
  - **Schedule Builder**: drag-and-drop tool that helps you create different schedule options.  
  - **Look Up Classes**: browse courses by subject, time, and availability.  
- You’ll enter **CRNs (Course Reference Numbers)** or use Schedule Builder to load classes directly.  

[Registration Tools – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registeringCourses/)  

---

## Confirming Your Schedule
- After adding courses, click **Submit** to finalize your schedule.  
- Always double-check:  
  - Class times don’t overlap.  
  - Labs and recitations are paired correctly with lectures.  
  - You’re meeting prerequisites.  
- Print or save a copy of your confirmed schedule as a backup.  

---

## Common Registration Errors
- **Pre-requisite not met** – some courses require prior classes.  
- **Time conflict** – overlapping class times.  
- **Closed/Waitlisted course** – course has no open seats (see [21.30.04 Waitlists & Course Overrides](21.30.04%20Waitlists.md)).  
- **Registration hold** – financial, advising, or immunization issue blocking enrollment.  

[Registration FAQs – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/registeringCourses/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Build a **backup schedule**—popular courses fill fast.  
- Register **as soon as your time ticket opens**.  
- Keep CRNs for all classes handy to speed things up.  
- Don’t panic if you miss a class—you can adjust during add/drop.
