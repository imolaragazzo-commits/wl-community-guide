## What Are Interdisciplinary Programs?
- Programs that pull courses from two or more colleges.  
- Designed for students with interests that span traditional disciplines.  
- Often housed in the **College of Liberal Arts**, **College of Science**, or the **Polytechnic Institute**, but with broad collaboration.  

---

## Examples of Interdisciplinary Majors
- **Data Science** (joint program between the College of Science and Polytechnic Institute).  
- **Environmental & Ecological Engineering** (College of Engineering + Agriculture + Science).  
- **Health & Human Sciences interdisciplinary programs** combining nutrition, psychology, and public health.  
- **Multidisciplinary Studies** (build-your-own path with advisor guidance).  

[Majors at Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/majors/)

## Specialized & Cross-Campus Programs
- **Degree+**: Earn two degrees in four years (e.g., Liberal Arts + another field).  
- **Cornerstone Integrated Liberal Arts**: Certificate program focusing on communication, ethics, and global issues.  
- **Entrepreneurship & Innovation Certificate**: Open to all majors, integrates business and creativity.  
- **Honors College**: Interdisciplinary approach with small classes and research.  

[Degree+ Program – Purdue](https://cla.purdue.edu/undergrad/degreeplus/)  
[Cornerstone Liberal Arts – Purdue](https://cla.purdue.edu/academic/cornerstone/)  
[Entrepreneurship & Innovation Certificate](https://www.purdue.edu/entr/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Interdisciplinary programs are great for **customizing your education**, but planning is key to graduate on time.  
- Check carefully how requirements overlap across colleges—double-counting may be limited.  
- Certificates like **Entrepreneurship & Innovation** can be paired with almost any major.
