## What is a Minor?
- A **secondary area of study** outside your major.  
- Usually requires **15–18 credits**.  
- Can be related to your career goals or a personal interest.  
- Appears on your transcript but not your diploma.  

[Minors at Purdue – Catalog](https://catalog.purdue.edu/content.php?catoid=16&navoid=20157)  

---

## What is a Certificate?
- A **focused program** that develops expertise in a specific skill or topic.  
- Often requires **12–18 credits**.  
- Examples: Entrepreneurship & Innovation Certificate, Collaborative Leadership Certificate, Applications in Data Science.  
- Certificates also appear on your transcript.  

[Certificate Programs – Purdue Catalog](https://catalog.purdue.edu/content.php?catoid=16&navoid=20157)  

---

## How to Add a Minor or Certificate
1. Review the official requirements in the **Purdue Catalog**.  
2. Meet with your academic advisor to discuss fit with your degree plan.  
3. Submit a request through your **college advising office** or via myPurdue (depending on program).  
4. Complete all courses with required grades.  
5. Confirm completion in **DegreeWorks/MyPurduePlan** before graduation.  

---

## Popular Minors & Certificates
- **Minors**: Psychology, Political Science, Management, Spanish, Computer Science.  
- **Certificates**: Entrepreneurship & Innovation, Leadership, Global Competence, Data Science.  
- Some minors/certificates are **open to all majors**, while others are **restricted** to certain colleges.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Plan early—courses may overlap with your core or elective requirements.  
- Adding a minor/certificate may **extend time to degree** if not planned carefully.  
- Check if your college allows double counting of credits (some limit overlap).
