At Purdue, switching majors is done through the **CODO process** (Change of Degree Objective). Each college sets its own requirements, and admission isn’t guaranteed—especially for competitive majors.

## What is CODO?
- A formal process for switching from one Purdue major to another.  
- Overseen by the **Registrar** and the target college’s advising office.  
- Students must meet GPA, coursework, and credit requirements set by the new program.  

[CODO Information – Purdue Registrar](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/students/ChangeCurriculum.html)  

---

## CODO Requirements
- Vary by program; check the official **CODO Requirements List** for details.  
- Common requirements include:  
  - Minimum cumulative GPA.  
  - Completion of prerequisite courses (e.g., math, science, writing).  
  - Minimum credit hours completed at Purdue.  
- Some majors admit only a limited number of CODO students each semester.  

[CODO Requirements by Major](https://www.purdue.edu/registrar/currentStudents/students/CODOMajorChange.html)  

---

## Steps in the CODO Process
1. Review the requirements for your intended major.  
2. Meet with your current academic advisor.  
3. Meet with an advisor in the new college/program.  
4. Submit the CODO application through the Registrar.  
5. Wait for official decision and update in myPurdue.  

---

## Competitive & Restricted Majors
- Majors such as **Computer Science, Nursing, and Professional Flight** are extremely competitive.  
- These programs may rarely (or never) accept CODO students.  
- For restricted programs, students are advised to apply directly as incoming freshmen.  

---

## Quick Tips
- Plan early—if you’re even considering switching majors, take courses that overlap across programs.  
- Have a backup plan in case your intended CODO program is closed.  
- Always talk to both your current and prospective advisors before applying.
