## College of Agriculture
- Known for programs in agronomy, animal sciences, food science, and agricultural economics.  
- Strong ties to Indiana’s agricultural industry and global food security.  
[College of Agriculture](https://ag.purdue.edu/)  

---

## College of Education
- Focuses on teacher preparation, curriculum development, and educational psychology.  
- Offers undergraduate majors in elementary and social studies education, plus licensure programs.  
[College of Education](https://education.purdue.edu/)  

---

## College of Engineering
- Purdue’s largest and most recognized college, consistently ranked nationally.  
- All first-year students enter **First-Year Engineering** before choosing a discipline (mechanical, electrical, aerospace, etc.).  
[College of Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/)  

---

## College of Health & Human Sciences
- Combines health, hospitality, and human development fields.  
- Includes nursing, health sciences, nutrition, hospitality & tourism, and psychological sciences.  
[College of Health & Human Sciences](https://www.purdue.edu/hhs/)  

---

## College of Liberal Arts
- Offers majors in the humanities, social sciences, and the arts.  
- Home to programs like political science, communication, history, English, and visual/performing arts.  
[College of Liberal Arts](https://cla.purdue.edu/)  

---

## College of Pharmacy
- Professional degree program (PharmD) plus undergraduate pre-pharmacy coursework.  
- Highly competitive admissions with limited seats.  
[College of Pharmacy](https://www.pharmacy.purdue.edu/)  

---

## College of Science
- Includes majors in biology, chemistry, computer science, mathematics, physics, and statistics.  
- Known for research opportunities and preparation for grad school.  
[College of Science](https://www.purdue.edu/science/)  

---

## College of Veterinary Medicine
- Offers the Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM), vet tech programs, and animal health studies.  
- Small, selective program with strong ties to animal hospitals and clinics.  
[College of Veterinary Medicine](https://vet.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Daniels School of Business
- Formerly the Krannert School, renamed in 2023.  
- Offers majors in management, finance, accounting, supply chain, and economics.  
[Daniels School of Business](https://business.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Polytechnic Institute
- Focuses on applied technology, aviation, computer and IT, construction management, and UX design.  
- Project-based learning with strong industry partnerships.  
[Polytechnic Institute](https://polytechnic.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Honors College
- John Martinson Honors College is a cross-disciplinary residential college.  
- Offers enhanced courses, research opportunities, and a strong living-learning community.  
[Honors College](https://honors.purdue.edu/)  

---

## Quick Tips
- Engineering, Nursing, and Computer Science are among the most competitive majors at admission.  
- Each college has its own **advising office** and student success center.  
- Explore beyond your major—many students pick up **minors or certificates** in other colleges.
